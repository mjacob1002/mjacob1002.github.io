Excited to announce the release of [TraceLab](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30560), a trace of ~4,300 real coding-agent sessions (~350K LLM steps, ~430K tool calls) from day-to-day use of Claude Code and Codex, along with an analysis of what these workloads mean for LLM serving! Check out the [website](https://tracelab.cs.washington.edu/) and the [announcement thread](https://x.com/UWSyFi/status/2071645652044419165) from UW SyFI!
